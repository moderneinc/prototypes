●  SECURITY

Closing the security gap Mythos-class models just widened

Mythos-class models find vulnerabilities faster than teams can close them, with tens of thousands not yet reaching the CVE database. Telling an agent to “go fix this CVE” can’t prove it caught every instance hiding behind a wrapper, a transitive dependency, or one of hundreds of other repos.

●  PRODUCT · LSTS

Mass ingest LSTs for currency, cadence, and consistency

Up-to-date, estate-wide LSTs power your security response, initiative planning, and real-time context for agents. A few things to keep in mind:

●Build LSTs centrally, not repo by repo. A centralized build keeps everything on the same versions instead of scattering work across CI systems.
●Source changes aren’t the only trigger. Dependency, CLI, or parser changes may require rebuilding all your LSTs.
●Mass ingest is how you do it. The reference covers why mass ingest keeps your estate current.

●  GUIDE · MIGRATION

New guide: Migrate to Jakarta EE 10

The javax→jakarta namespace shift breaks binary compatibility, so a find-and-replace across imports, POMs, and XML config almost always ends in compilation errors. The new guide walks through the composite recipe that updates packages, deprecated APIs, build plugins, and Faces/JSF namespaces — then shows how to inspect the diff and finish anything that needs a human eye.
