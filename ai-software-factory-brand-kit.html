AI Software Factory

The event identity for the AI Software Factory Summit and the AI Software Factory Meetup series. This is the working brand reference — the logo lock-up, color, type, and the rules for putting them together. Two applications live here: the Summit (flagship, city-based) and the Meetups (partner-hosted, local).

Color

Core + accents

Four core colors carry the system — teal holds the AI SOFTWARE FACTORY block, blue the qualifier block and live accent, midnight for ink and dark canvas, and slate for every block's text and light surfaces. Four extended accents — plum, cyan, magenta, and grey — are available for data, illustration, and social.

Gradients

Two-gradient system

Two gradients live in the visual language, chosen by placement. Gradient 01 is used when the gradient overlays imagery in “Hard Light” blend mode; Gradient 02 is used when the gradient sits on a white background. Either can suit a given visual field, and the two can be combined in one field for added depth and dimension.

Typography

Space Grotesk · Space Mono

Space Grotesk is the display face — it sets AI SOFTWARE FACTORY and headlines, uppercase with 6% letter-spacing. Space Mono is the technical face — it sets the qualifier (meet up, summit miami), the year, dates, locations, and any label.

Using the brand

Do & don't

Do

  • Keep the two-block stack: teal AI SOFTWARE FACTORY over the blue qualifier.
  • Set the wordmark in Space Grotesk at 6% tracking; the qualifier and year in Space Mono.
  • Use summit [city] for the summit and meet up for meetups.
  • Overlay the lock-up on full-bleed photography — palms/tropical for the summit, city skylines for meetups.
  • Co-brand with the Moderne mark (and partner logo) locked up separately with a “+”.
  • Keep all block text #F8F8FF white.

Don't

  • Recolor the blocks or swap teal/blue roles.
  • Set the wordmark type on a photo without its teal block behind it.
  • Stretch, rotate, outline, or add shadows to the lock-up.
  • Mix in other typefaces — only Space Grotesk and Space Mono.
  • Crowd the lock-up; always hold one block-height of clear space.
  • Use low-contrast tints of the palette for block fills.

Photography

Tropical & Miami skyline

The summit's imagery is bright, high-key photography — tropical palms and the Miami skyline, shot from below against blue sky. A spectral-gradient wash (the strand spectrum) can be laid over the photo for cover art and social; keep it subtle so the scene still reads. Always set the lock-up inside its solid teal block over the photo — never place brand type directly on the image.
